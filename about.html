About DeRivian

Our journey to exceptional care

DeRivian Care Ltd is a London-based provider specialising in high-quality domiciliary care for adults in their own homes. We exist to deliver personal care that keeps people safe, well and independent — where dignity and choice remain central to every moment of every day.

Built on the values of Care, Comfort and Compassion, we combine extensive experience in care coordination, safeguarding and regulatory compliance with a genuinely human approach. The result is care that is safe, responsive and outcome-focused — and built entirely around the person receiving it.

Our foundation

The values that guide our work each day

We built DeRivian on three pillars. These aren't words on a wall — they're the reason we show up, the way we listen, and how we treat every person we support.

Care

We learn from every interaction and never stop improving. Care that adapts to meet each person exactly where they are, today and as their needs change.

Comfort

Independence is the real goal. Our support exists to help people do what matters most to them — safely, confidently and in the comfort of their own home.

Compassion

We honour the choices, culture and preferences of those we care for — with respect in every moment, even when the harder path is the right one.

Excellence

The DeRivian way sets us apart

We invest deeply in our staff because they are the heart of everything we do. Strong, supported teams deliver the consistent, compassionate care that families trust and people deserve.
